Types of carbohydrates

أنواع الكربوهيدرات

The following diagram shows the most important types of carbohydrates, according to their chemical composition (molecular structure):

Carbohydrates are divided into

Monosaccharides

The simplest form of carbohydrates.

🍇 Glucose (grape sugar)🍑 Fructose (fruit sugar)

Disaccharides

Sugars in which each molecule consists of two monosaccharide units with one water molecule removed.

Sucrose (cane sugar) — one unit of glucose + one unit of fructose
Maltose (malt sugar) — two units of glucose

Oligosaccharides

Sugars consist of 3 : 9 units of monosaccharides.

🫘 Raffinose (legume sugar)

Polysaccharides

Sugars consist of more than 9 units of monosaccharides.

🌽 Starch🌿 Cellulose
الكربوهيدرات ٤ أنواع: أحادية (الجلوكوز والفركتوز)، ثنائية (السكروز = جلوكوز + فركتوز، المالتوز = جلوكوز + جلوكوز مع حذف جزيء ماء)، قليلة التعدد (٣:٩ وحدات زي الرافينوز)، ومتعددة التعدد (أكتر من ٩ وحدات زي النشا والسليلوز).

Detection of glucose

الكشف عن الجلوكوز

• Glucose can be detected using blue Benedict's solution, as shown in the following activity:

Activity ① : Detection of glucose sugar — Steps:

  1. Place 2 mL of glucose sugar solution at different concentrations in 3 test tubes, and place only the same volume of water in a fourth tube.
  2. Add 2 mL of Benedict's solution to each tube.
  3. Heat the test tubes in a water bath for 5 minutes.
Observation: The colour of the blue Benedict's solution (reagent) changes to several different colours with glucose solutions, and its colour does not change with water.
Conclusion: The colour of the glucose solution changes when Benedict's solution is added to it, depending on the concentration of sugar in the solution, as shown in the opposite table:
RedHigh
OrangeMedium
GreenLow
BlueNone
Resulting colourRedOrangeGreenBlue
Concentration of glucose sugar solutionHighMediumLowNone
بنحط ٢ مللي جلوكوز بتراكيز مختلفة في ٣ أنابيب والرابعة ماء، بنضيف بندكت أزرق ونسخّن ٥ دقايق. اللون: أحمر = عالي، برتقالي = متوسط، أخضر = منخفض، أزرق = مفيش.

Integration with Biology — Diabetes

التكامل مع الأحياء — السكري

• Cause of the disease:

The accumulation of glucose sugar in blood, as a result of insufficient secretion of insulin hormone, which is secreted by the pancreas.

• Symptoms of the disease:

① Significant weight loss.② Increased urination times.③ Excessive thirst.
🔎 Note: Benedict's reagent can be used when diabetes is suspected by adding drops of it to a sample of the urine of the person suspected of having the disease and observing the resulting colour.
السكري بيحصل بتراكم الجلوكوز في الدم لنقص الأنسولين من البنكرياس. الأعراض: نقص وزن، كثرة تبول، عطش شديد. دوس على الأعضاء في الرسمة تعرف وظيفتها، وجرب زر الفك 🧩.

Detection of starch

الكشف عن النشا

• Starch can be detected using a yellowish-brown iodine solution, as shown in the following activity:

Activity ② : Detection of starch — Steps:

  1. Cut a potato tuber in half.
  2. Add drops of iodine solution to the inner part of the potato.
Observation: The yellowish-brown colour of the iodine changes to dark blue.
Conclusion: The starch in the potatoes changes the colour of the iodine solution from yellowish brown to dark blue.
بننقط يود على بطاطس مقطوعة، اللون بيتحول أزرق غامق — ده كشف النشا.

Second: Fats

ثانياً: الدهون
Fats: Organic chemical compounds that contain the same elements as carbohydrates.

• The chemical composition of both fats and carbohydrates is similar… G.R.?
Because each of them consists of the following elements:

• Carbon (C)
• Hydrogen (H)
• Oxygen (O)

From the sources of fats in food

🧈Butter
🥛Milk
🥩Meat
🫒Vegetable oils
🥚Eggs
🥜Many types of nuts

∗ Carbohydrates and fats are essential chemical compounds for the transport and storage of energy in living organisms. Despite the similarity in the chemical composition of both fats and carbohydrates, fats provide the body with more energy than carbohydrates.

Q

Which provides the body with more energy (20 g of carbohydrates or 20 g of fats)? With explanation.

A 20 g of fats, because fats provide the body with more energy than carbohydrates.
الدهون فيها نفس عناصر الكربوهيدرات بالظبط (كربون وهيدروجين وأكسجين) عشان كده تركيبهما الكيميائي متشابه. بس الدهون بتدي الجسم طاقة أكتر — يعني ٢٠ جم دهون طاقتها أكبر من ٢٠ جم كربوهيدرات. ومصادرها: الزبدة واللحوم والبيض واللبن والزيوت النباتية والمكسرات.

Medical awareness: Cholesterol

وعي طبي: الكوليسترول

▸ Cholesterol is a fatty substance found with body fats.

Cholesterol: A fatty substance its deposition on the inside walls of arteries leads to their blockage.

▸ Cholesterol risks:

The deposition of cholesterol inside the arteries leads to their blockagetherefore ➜The risk of heart diseases increases.

▸ To maintain a stable normal level of cholesterol in blood, it requires the following:

1🚫Reduce your intake of fats and fried foods.
2🏃Exercise regularly.
3💧Drink plenty of water.
الكوليسترول مادة دهنية بتترسب على الجدران الداخلية للشرايين فتسدّها — دوس على الشريان المسدود والسليم والقلب في الرسمة تشوف الفرق — وعشان نحافظ على مستوى طبيعي: نقلل من الدهون والمقليات، نتمرن بانتظام، ونشرب مية كتير.

Question ? Answer — Excess carbohydrates

سؤال وجواب — زيادة الكربوهيدرات
Q

What happens when you consume more carbohydrates than the body needs?

A The excess is stored in:
- The liver and muscles in the form of glycogen.
- The body's cells in the form of fats that are burned when needed, such as during periods of fasting or when following diets.
لو أكلت كربوهيدرات زيادة عن احتياج الجسم، الزيادة بتتخزن: في الكبد والعضلات على هيئة جلايكوجين، وفي خلايا الجسم على هيئة دهون بتتحرق وقت الاحتياج زي فترات الصيام أو الرجيم.

Detection of fats

الكشف عن الدهون

∗ Fats can be detected using red Sudan (IV) solution, as shown in the following activity:

Activity ③ : Detection of fats — Steps:

  1. Separate the egg yolk (yellow) "rich in fat" from the albumen (egg white).
  2. Add the egg yolk to distilled water and shake to make a mixture.
  3. Add 10 mL of Sudan (IV) solution to 10 mL of the previous mixture with shaking.
Observation: A red-orange colour is formed on the surface of the mixture.
Conclusion: Sudan (IV) solution gives a red-orange colour on the surfaces of mixtures that contain fat.
Adding Sudan (IV) solution
بنفصل الصفار (الغني بالدهن) عن البياض، بنرجّه في ماء مقطر، وبنضيف ١٠ مللي من محلول سودان (٤) الأحمر على ١٠ مللي من المخلوط مع الرج — هتتكون طبقة حمراء برتقالية على السطح، وده دليل وجود الدهون.

Third: Proteins

ثالثاً: البروتينات
Proteins: Organic chemical compounds consisting of water-soluble basic units, called amino acids.

∗ Enzymes help in converting amino acids into proteins.

∗ The amino acids that make up proteins are composed of the same elements found in both carbohydrates and fats, which are:

• Carbon (C)
• Hydrogen (H)
• Oxygen (O)
• In addition to nitrogen (N)

From the sources of proteins in food

🥩Red meat
🍗White meat
🐟Fish
🥚Eggs
🥛Milk and its derivatives
🫘Legumes
🥜Nuts
🌱Soybeans
🔎 Note: Proteins are not considered a major source of energy.
G.R.? The danger of excessive use of dietary supplements containing amino acids for the purpose of rapid muscle building.
Because they cause serious risks to the kidneys and liver.
البروتينات مركبات عضوية وحداتها الأساسية أحماض أمينية ذائبة في الماء، والإنزيمات بتساعد في تحويلها لبروتينات. عناصرها نفس عناصر الدهون والكربوهيدرات + النيتروجين (N). مصادرها حيوانية (لحوم حمراء وبيضا وسمك وبيض ولبن) ونباتية (بقوليات ومكسرات وصويا). والبروتينات مش مصدر رئيسي للطاقة. واحذر المكملات الغذائية لبناء العضلات لأنها بتضر الكلى والكبد.

Detection of proteins

الكشف عن البروتينات

∗ Proteins can be detected using a Biuret solution (blue in colour), as shown in the following activity:

Activity ④ : Detection of proteins — Steps:

  1. Separate the egg albumen (egg white) from the yolk (egg yellow).
  2. Put a portion of the egg white (rich in protein) in a test tube.
  3. Add a few drops of Biuret solution to the albumen with a gentle shake.
Observation: After shaking, the blue colour of the Biuret solution changes to violet.
Conclusion: Biuret solution forms a violet colour when added to proteins.
بنحط بياض البيض في أنبوبة، بنضيف نقط محلول بيوريت الأزرق مع رج خفيف — اللون بيتحول من الأزرق للبنفسجي، وده دليل وجود البروتين.

The importance of proteins

أهمية البروتينات

There are many ways in which living organisms benefit from proteins, such as:

1They are involved in building muscles, and the repair and growth of damaged cells.
2They are used in the formation of enzymes that act as catalysts.
3They are used in the formation of antibodies that support the immune system against disease-causing microbes.
Proteins are involved in the repair and growth of damaged cells
PROTEIN IS ESSENTIAL FOR REPAIR & GROWTH: Provides amino acids to rebuild skin, muscle, and connective tissue · Supports collagen formation for healing · Fuels cell regeneration and strength. Key: Protein → Tissue Repair → Growth

The benefits of proteins extend to many industries, such as:

1Extraction of gelatin which is used in the food industry from the proteins found in the bones and skins of animals.
2Production of insulin which is used in the treatment of diabetes through genetic engineering.
3The use of enzymes in the manufacture of laundry detergents to remove stains.
البروتينات بتبني العضلات وبتصلح الخلايا التالفة، وبتكوِّن الإنزيمات والأجسام المضادة للمناعة. وصناعياً: جيلاتين من عظام وجلود الحيوانات، أنسولين لمرضى السكري بالهندسة الوراثية، وإنزيمات في مساحيق الغسيل لإزالة البقع.
