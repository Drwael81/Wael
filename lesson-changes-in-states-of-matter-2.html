💨 Evaporation vs 🔥 Boiling

The difference between evaporation and boiling / الفرق بين التبخر والغليان

💨 Evaporation process

  1. The process of conversion of matter from liquid to gas at any temperature less than its boiling point.
    تحول السائل لغاز عند أي درجة حرارة أقل من درجة الغليان.
  2. It occurs to molecules on the surface of the liquid.
    بيحصل لجزيئات سطح السائل بس.
  3. It occurs by gaining thermal energy from the surrounding medium or from a heat source.
    بيكتسب طاقة حرارية من الوسط المحيط أو مصدر حرارة.
  4. It takes a relatively long period of time.
    بياخد وقت طويل نسبيًا.
  5. It is not accompanied by the formation of air bubbles (gas inside liquid).
    مفيش فقاعات هواء جوه السائل.

🔥 Boiling process

  1. The process of conversion of matter from liquid to gas at a certain point, known as the boiling point.
    تحول السائل لغاز عند نقطة معينة اسمها درجة الغليان.
  2. It occurs to all molecules of the liquid.
    بيحصل لكل جزيئات السائل مش السطح بس.
  3. It occurs by gaining thermal energy from a heat source.
    بيكتسب طاقة حرارية من مصدر حرارة بس.
  4. It takes a relatively short period of time.
    بياخد وقت قصير نسبيًا.
  5. It is accompanied by the formation of air bubbles (gas inside liquid).
    بتتكون فقاعات هواء جوه السائل.

⚙️ Factors Affecting Evaporation

Factors affecting the rate of evaporation of liquids / العوامل المؤثرة على معدل التبخر

🧠 Cumulative Knowledge: Types of Variables / أنواع المتغيرات

🔒

Controlled variable

The factor that remains constant during the experiment.

العامل اللي بيفضل ثابت طول التجربة.
🎛️

Independent variable (Cause)

The factor that is changed during the experiment.

العامل اللي بنغيّره إحنا.
📈

Dependent variable (Effect)

The factor that changes depending on the independent variable.

العامل اللي بيتغير نتيجة تغيير المستقل.

🌡️ Factor 1: Temperature

Controlled Surface area of the liquid – Humidity percentage – Air currents.
مساحة سطح السائل - نسبة الرطوبة - تيارات الهواء (كلهم ثابتين).
Independent Difference in temperature.
الفرق في درجة الحرارة بين الوعاءين.
Dependent The evaporation rate in container (2) is greater than that in container (1).
معدل التبخر في الوعاء (2) أعلى من الوعاء (1).
Conclusion The evaporation rate increases by increasing temperature.
معدل التبخر بيزيد بزيادة درجة الحرارة.
Explanation The evaporation rate increases due to increasing the number of molecules that have sufficient thermal energy from the surrounding medium to overcome the attractive forces of other molecules and escape from the surface of the liquid into the air.
لأن زيادة الحرارة بتزود عدد الجزيئات اللي عندها طاقة كافية تتغلب على قوى التجاذب وتهرب من سطح السائل.

📐 Factor 2: Surface area of the liquid exposed to evaporation

Controlled Temperature – Humidity percentage – Air currents.
درجة الحرارة - نسبة الرطوبة - تيارات الهواء.
Independent Difference in the surface area of the liquid.
الفرق في مساحة سطح السائل بين الوعاءين.
Dependent The evaporation rate in container (1) is greater than that in container (2).
معدل التبخر في الوعاء (1) أعلى من الوعاء (2).
Conclusion The evaporation rate increases by increasing the surface area of the liquid exposed to evaporation.
معدل التبخر بيزيد بزيادة مساحة السطح المعرّضة للتبخر.
Explanation The evaporation rate increases due to increasing the number of molecules that can gain the required thermal energy from the surrounding medium to be released from the surface of the liquid.
لأن زيادة المساحة بتزود عدد الجزيئات اللي ممكن تاخد الطاقة اللازمة وتتحرر من السطح.

🔄 Reversibility of State Changes

Matter changes (conversions) are reversible processes / تحولات المادة عمليات عكسية

Melting and freezing are two reversible processes, and evaporation and condensation are also two reversible processes.

🧊 ⇌ 💧
Melting / Freezing
💧 ⇌ 💨
Evaporation / Condensation
كل التحولات بين حالات المادة تغير فيزيائي مقلوب — جزيئات الماء بتفضل زي ما هي ومبتتحولش لهيدروجين وأكسچين.
Note: The conversion of water from one state to another is a physical change — water molecules (H₂O) do not transform into hydrogen (H₂) and oxygen (O₂) molecules.

🌫️ Sublimation & ❄️ Deposition

🌫️ Sublimation

The change of a substance directly from the solid state to the gaseous state without passing through the liquid state.

تحول المادة من صلب لغاز مباشرة من غير ما تعدي بالسائل.

Example: Dry ice sublimes when it absorbs heat from the surrounding medium. Dry ice is carbon dioxide in its solid state.

الثلج الجاف (dry ice) هو ثاني أكسيد الكربون في حالته الصلبة، وبيتسامى وهو بياخد حرارة من الوسط المحيط.

❄️ Deposition

The change of a substance directly from the gaseous state to the solid state without passing through the liquid state.

تحول المادة من غاز لصلب مباشرة من غير ما تعدي بالسائل.

Example: Frost is formed on the surfaces of crops and cars when the temperature of water vapour found in the air becomes below 0°C.

الصقيع بيتكون على النباتات والعربيات لما درجة حرارة بخار الماء في الهواء تقل عن 0°م.

☕ Technological Application: Instant Coffee

Regular coffee is made from grinding coffee beans that does not dissolve in water, while instant coffee dissolves quickly in water.

القهوة العادية من طحن البن ومش بتدوب، أما سريعة الذوبان فبتدوب بسرعة.
1

Preparation

A concentrated extract is prepared from regular coffee.

بيتحضّر مستخلص مركّز من القهوة العادية.
2

Spraying & Heating

The sprayed concentrated coffee extract is exposed to extremely hot dry air (about 250°C).

المستخلص المركز بيترش ويتعرض لهواء ساخن جاف جدًا (حوالي 250°م).
💡 G.R.: to increase the surface area of the spray droplets exposed to the hot air to increase the evaporation rate.
السبب: زيادة مساحة سطح قطرات الرش المعرّضة للهواء الساخن عشان يزيد معدل التبخر.
3

Collection

The solid instant coffee crystals are collected.

بيتم تجميع بلورات القهوة سريعة الذوبان الصلبة.

تدريبات تفاعلية

Q1: Explain the following statements based on your understanding of the factors that affect the rate of evaporation:

(1) Wet clothes dry during the day when the sun is shining faster than at night.

Due to the higher temperature and lower humidity during the day than at night, the rate of evaporation increases, so wet clothes dry faster during the day.

بسبب ارتفاع الحرارة وانخفاض الرطوبة نهارًا، معدل التبخر بيزيد فالملابس تجف أسرع.

(2) Feeling uncomfortable in the hot humid weather.

In hot weather, sweat secretion increases to reduce the feeling of heat, but when humidity is high, the rate of evaporation of sweat decreases, causing discomfort.

لما الرطوبة تكون عالية، معدل تبخر العرق بيقل، فبيحصل شعور بعدم الارتياح.
Q2: The chart shows the change in the total mass of three identical graduated cylinders (X), (Y), (Z), each empty cylinder mass = 50 g, containing the same quantity of water, left for 30 minutes in different locations. Mark (✓) or (✗) with explanation:

📊 Chart Info: mass of X drops a little, Y drops more, Z reaches 50 g (completely dry) after 30 min.

  1. Water in the three cylinders dries up after 30 minutes. (✗) - Only Z dried up completely.
  2. Cylinder (Y) was placed in a sunny place, unlike cylinder (X). (✓) - Y lost more mass than X due to higher temperature.
  3. Cylinder (Z) is placed in a place exposed to air currents, unlike cylinder (Y). (✓) - Z dried up completely, indicating the highest evaporation rate due to air currents.
  4. The surface area of water exposed to evaporation in cylinder (X) is greater than that in cylinder (Z). (✗) - If X had a greater surface area, it would have evaporated more, but Z evaporated the most.

Q3: Three equal volumes of water were placed in three different-shaped containers. Which of the following represents the volumes of remaining water in each container after several hours?

🧪 Container (1): narrow base, wide top. | Container (2): very wide opening. | Container (3): wide base, narrow top.

مساحة السطح أصغر إيه في (3) وأكبر إيه في (2)، فأقل كمية اتبخرت من (3)، بعدين (1)، وأخيرًا (2).
