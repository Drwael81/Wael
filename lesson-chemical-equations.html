Symbolic chemical equation

المعادلة الكيميائية الرمزية

▸ The reaction of carbon combustion in atmospheric air to form carbon dioxide gas is expressed:

With the following word equation:

Carbon combustion in atmospheric air

But, this is not sufficient to express chemical reactions.
Because it does not clarify:

The ratio between the numbers of molecules or atoms present in the reaction mixture.
The physical state of the reaction substances.

Therefore: the chemical reaction is expressed by a symbolic chemical equation.

Symbolic chemical equation: A symbolic representation of a chemical reaction showing the reactants and products by using symbols and molecular formulas, with the simplest integer ratio of the number of atoms or molecules entering and resulting from the reaction.
احتراق الكربون بيتكتب كمعادلة كلمات: كربون + أكسجين ← (حرارة) ثاني أكسيد الكربون. بس معادلة الكلمات مش كفاية لأنها مش بتوضح نسب الذرات ولا الحالة الفيزيائية — عشان كده بنعبّر عن التفاعل بمعادلة رمزية.

Steps of writing a symbolic chemical equation

خطوات كتابة المعادلة الرمزية

▸ The symbolic chemical equation is written from left to right by following the steps below:

❶ Write symbols and formulas of reacting substances (reactants) on the left side of the equation
❷ 👇Link the reactants and the products with an arrow that indicates the direction of reaction and the conditions of reaction are written on it, if found
❸ 👇Write symbols and formulas of resulting substances (products) on the right side of the equation
٤ خطوات: المتفاعلات شمال، سهم اتجاه التفاعل وفوقه الشروط، النواتج يمين، وتحت كل رمز الحالة الفيزيائية. دوس التشغيل وشوف المعادلة بتتبني.

Laws of chemical combination

قوانين الاتحاد الكيميائي

▸ Chemical reactions obey the laws of chemical combination, including:

1Law of Conservation of Matter
2Law of Conservation of Mass
التفاعلات بتطيع قانونين: حفظ المادة وحفظ الكتلة.
🧪

1 · Law of Conservation of Matter

قانون حفظ المادة

▸ When a chemical reaction occurs:

The reacting substances (reactants) are neither destroyed, nor new substances (products) created from nothing.

Rather:

The atoms of the reactant molecules are rearranged to form new products with the same number of atoms of each element. Which fulfils the law of conservation of matter.

Law of Conservation of Matter: Matter is neither destroyed nor created from nothing, but it changes from one form to another.

🧑‍ Application:

▸ In the reaction of carbon combustion in atmospheric air: The carbon and oxygen atoms (the reactants) are rearranged in the same number to form carbon dioxide molecule (the products).

الذرات مش بتفنى ولا بتتخلق — بتتعاد ترتيبها بنفس العدد: ذرة كربون + ذرتا أكسجين ← جزيء CO₂ واحد.

2 · Law of Conservation of Mass

قانون حفظ الكتلة

▸ The following activity is carried out to clarify the concept of the law of conservation of mass.

Activity : Law of Conservation of Mass — Steps:

  1. Place a flask containing sodium sulphate solution Na₂SO₄ on the balance.
  2. Carefully place a test tube containing calcium chloride solution CaCl₂ inside the flask … G.R.? To avoid mixing the two solutions.
  3. Cover the flask's mouth with a stopper and record the total mass of the flask and its contents.
  4. Turn the flask upside down (is inverted) … G.R.? To let the two solutions react together.
  5. Record the mass of the flask again after the occurrence of the reaction.
  6. Compare the total mass before and after the reaction.
Observation: The total mass of the substances before the reaction (Na₂SO₄, CaCl₂) is equal to the total mass of the substances after the reaction (CaSO₄, NaCl).
Conclusion: The mass of the reactants does not change despite the occurrence of a chemical reaction accompanied by the formation of a precipitate of calcium sulphate. This indicates that chemical reactions obey the Law of Conservation of Mass.
Law of Conservation of Mass: The total mass of the substances involved in the chemical reaction is equal to the total mass of the substances resulting from it.

Balanced symbolic equation

المعادلة الرمزية الموزونة

∗ A symbolic equation is described as balanced when the numbers of atoms of the elements in reactants are equal to their numbers in the products, which fulfils the law of conservation of mass.

Example:

Which of the two following figures (1) and (2) represents the balanced symbolic equation for the combustion reaction of methane gas CH₄ in the presence of oxygen gas O₂ to form carbon dioxide gas CO₂ and water vapour H₂O?

∗ The following table shows the numbers of atoms of reactants and products of the reaction in each of the two previous figures:

ElementFigure (1)Figure (2)
ProductsReactantsProductsReactants
C1=11=1
H2≠44=4
O3≠24=4

From this, it is obvious that:

∗ Equation: CH₄ + O₂ —Δ→ CO₂ + H₂O is unbalanced … G.R.? Because the numbers (masses) of carbon (C), hydrogen (H) and oxygen (O) atoms are not equal on both sides of the equation.
∗ Equation: CH₄ + 2O₂ —Δ→ CO₂ + 2H₂O is balanced … G.R.? Because the numbers (masses) of carbon (C), hydrogen (H) and oxygen (O) atoms are equal on both sides of the equation.
Balanced symbolic equation: A symbolic chemical equation in which the number of atoms of each element in the reactants is equal to the number of atoms of the same element in the products.
المعادلة الموزونة هي اللي عدد ذرات كل عنصر فيها متساوي في المتفاعلات والنواتج (زي شكل ٢: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O) وده بيحقق قانون حفظ الكتلة. شكل (١) مش موزون لأن H وO مش متساويين. وزن المعادلة زي ضبط الكفتين على الميزان: بنعدّل المعاملات بس. دوس على زر الوزن وشوف الكفة بتتعدل لما بنضيف المعامل ٢.

Coefficients & subscripts

المعاملات والأسس

There are two types of numbers in the symbolic equation that represents the methane combustion reaction, which are: Coefficients and Subscripts

Coefficients: The numbers that precede the symbols of the elements or the molecular formulas of the compounds in the balanced symbolic equation.
Subscripts: The numbers found below the symbols of the elements forming the molecule and represent the number of atoms in one molecule.

🔎 Important notes — In the balanced symbolic equation:

① The coefficients represent the simplest ratio by which the reactants interact to form the products.

② The number 1 is not written for either the coefficients or the subscripts.

③ In the balanced symbolic equation, the sum of the coefficients on both sides of the equation is not necessarily equal, as shown in the balanced equation: 2 Mg + 1 O₂ ⟶ 2 MgO → 2 + 1 = 3 ≠ 2

④ The number of atoms of each element in the reactants or products is calculated by multiplying the coefficient by the subscript.

🧑‍🔬 Application: 2H₂O → Number of hydrogen atoms = 2 × 2 = 4, Number of oxygen atoms = 2 × 1 = 2
المعاملات أرقام زرقاء قبل الرموز (بتعدل الوزن)، والأسس أرقام خضرا تحت الرموز (بتقول عدد الذرات في الجزيء). ملحوظات مهمة: المعاملات أبسط نسبة، والرقم ١ مش بيتكتب، ومجموع المعاملات في الطرفين مش لازم يتساوى (2+1≠2)، وعدد الذرات = المعامل × الأس (زي 2H₂O فيها ٤ هيدروجين و٢ أكسجين).

How to balance a symbolic chemical equation

طريقة وزن المعادلة الرمزية

∗ The symbolic equation is balanced by applying the law of conservation of mass, according to the following steps, which are illustrated by balancing the equation of the reaction of nitrogen gas N₂ with hydrogen gas H₂ to form ammonia gas NH₃:

Step ①Write the correct molecular formulas for both the reactants and products in the form of a symbolic equation.
N₂(g) + H₂(g) ⟶ NH₃(g)
Step ②Compare the number of atoms of each element in the reactants with the number of atoms of the same element in the products.
ElementN: 2 ≠ 1H: 2 ≠ 3

This equation is unbalanced, because the number of nitrogen and hydrogen atoms in the reactants is not equal to their number in the products.

Step ③If the numbers of atoms are not equal, one of the coefficients is adjusted, and the numbers of atoms are compared again.

▸ To equalize the number of nitrogen atoms, multiply the coefficient of NH₃ × 2: N₂(g) + H₂(g) ⟶ 2NH₃(g) → N: 2 = 2 ✓ but H: 2 ≠ 6 → still unbalanced (hydrogen in reactants is less than products).

In case of repeated inequality, the coefficients are adjusted again, even more than once.

▸ To equalize the number of hydrogen atoms, multiply the coefficient of H₂ × 3: N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⟶ 2NH₃(g) → N: 2 = 2 ✓ , H: 6 = 6 ✓

Step ④Check the balance of equation by ensuring that the numbers of atoms of each element on both sides of the equation are equal.
N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⟶ 2NH₃(g)
NH
Reactants1 × 2 = 23 × 2 = 6
Products2 × 1 = 22 × 3 = 6

The equation becomes balanced, since the numbers of N and H atoms in the reactants and products are equal.

G.R.? The symbolic equation must be balanced, in order to fulfil the law of conservation of matter.
الوزن بيتم على ٤ خطوات بتطبيق قانون حفظ الكتلة: ١) نكتب الصيغ الصحيحة، ٢) نقارن عدد الذرات (طلعت مش متساوية فالمعادلة مش موزونة)، ٣) نعدّل المعاملات: ضربنا NH₃ في ٢ عشان النيتروجين، وبعدين H₂ في ٣ عشان الهيدروجين، ٤) نتأكد: N: 1×2=2×1 وH: 3×2=2×3 — المعادلة اتوزنت. دوس التشغيل وشوف الخطوات بتضيء بالترتيب.

Example — Atomic groups (NO₃)

مثال — المجموعات الذرية

Example:

Rewrite the equation after balancing it: Mg + AgNO₃ ⟶ Mg(NO₃)₂ + Ag

Idea of solution:

Before balancing the equation: If there is an atomic group (such as: nitrate group NO₃⁻) on both sides of the equation, it is considered as one unit, not as individual elements.

In the reactantsAg(NO₃) → One unit
In the productsMg(NO₃)₂ → Two units

To equalize the number of NO₃ units, the coefficient of AgNO₃ is multiplied × 2 : Mg + 2AgNO₃ ⟶ Mg(NO₃)₂ + Ag

To equalize the number of Ag atoms, the coefficient of Ag is multiplied × 2 : Mg + 2AgNO₃ ⟶ Mg(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag

A: Mg + 2AgNO₃ ⟶ Mg(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag

🧑‍🔬 Evaluate Your Understanding:

Rewrite the following equation after balancing it, by following the same steps as in the previous example: KNO₃ + H₂CO₃ ⟶ K₂CO₃ + HNO₃

لو في مجموعة ذرية زي النترات (NO₃) موجودة في الطرفين، بنعتبرها وحدة واحدة مش عناصر منفصلة: في المتفاعلات وحدة واحدة وفي النواتج وحدتين، فضربنا AgNO₃ في ٢، وبعدين وزنّا الفضة بضرب Ag في ٢. جرّب بنفسك: 2KNO₃ + H₂CO₃ → K₂CO₃ + 2HNO₃.

Q2

Place the correct coefficients in each cell to fulfil the balancing of the following equations:

(1) Al + O₂ ⟶ Al₂O₃
(2) C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ ⟶ CO₂ + H₂O

Idea of solution:

(1) • To equalize the number of Al atoms, multiply the coefficient of Al × 2 : 2Al + O₂ ⟶ Al₂O₃
• To equalize the number of O atoms, multiply the coefficient of O × 3/2 : 2Al + 3/2 O₂ ⟶ Al₂O₃
In order to eliminate the fraction (3/2) in the coefficient of O₂, all the coefficients are multiplied by × 2 : 4Al + 3O₂ ⟶ 2Al₂O₃

(2) • To equalize the number of C atoms, multiply the coefficient of CO₂ × 6 : C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ ⟶ 6CO₂ + H₂O
• To equalize the number of H atoms, multiply the coefficient of H₂O × 6 : C₆H₁₂O₆ + O₂ ⟶ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O
• To equalize the number of O atoms, multiply the coefficient of O₂ × 6 : C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ ⟶ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O

A: (1) 4Al + 3O₂ ⟶ 2Al₂O₃ (2) C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ ⟶ 6CO₂ + 6H₂O
