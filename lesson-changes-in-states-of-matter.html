Changes in the States of Matter

How thermal energy reshapes ice, water, and vapour — and why the thermometer sometimes refuses to move.

01 — The Mechanism

What happens when matter gains heat

Three states, one rule: heat speeds particles up. But during an actual change of state, that extra energy stops raising temperature and starts breaking bonds instead.

Solid — Ice

Particles vibrate faster in fixed positions as heat is gained → temperature of the ice rises.

الجليد لما ياخد حرارة، الجزيئات بتهتز أسرع في مكانها، فدرجة حرارته بترتفع عادي.

Liquid — Water

At the melting point, attraction forces weaken and ice turns to liquid while temperature stays fixed at 0 °C until melting is complete.

عند نقطة الانصهار، قوى التجاذب بتضعف، فالجليد بيبقى ميه — والحرارة بتفضل ثابتة على 0°م لحد ما كل الجليد يدوب.

Gas — Vapour

Past the boiling point, kinetic energy keeps rising with heat — while temperature plateaus again at 100 °C until evaporation is complete.

بعد نقطة الغليان، الطاقة الحركية للجزيئات بتزيد، وبرضه الحرارة بتقف عند 100°م لحد ما الميه كلها تتبخر.

General rules

  • Gaining thermal energy → kinetic energy of particles increases → temperature rises (and vice versa).
  • A heated liquid keeps warming gradually until it hits its boiling point.
  • A cooling liquid keeps losing heat gradually until it hits its freezing point.
  • During melting and boiling, temperature stays constant — the absorbed energy is spent weakening attraction forces between particles, not heating them further.
يعني الخلاصة: الحرارة بتزوّد سرعة الجزيئات، لكن وقت التحول نفسه (دوبان أو غليان) الطاقة بتتصرف في كسر الروابط مش في رفع الحرارة — عشان كده الترمومتر "بيقف" لفترة.

03 — What Shifts The Curve

Factors affecting melting & boiling points

A pure substance has a fixed melting/boiling point only at normal atmospheric pressure. Two things move it: pressure, and purity.

FACTOR 01

Atmospheric pressure

At sea level, pressure is described as normal atmospheric pressure: ice melts at 0 °C and water boils at 100 °C. As altitude rises above sea level, atmospheric pressure drops below normal — and both fixed points shift.

  • Decreases below 100 °C as altitude increases.
  • Falls by about 1 °C for every 300 m of altitude gained.
  • Increases above 0 °C when atmospheric pressure decreases.
  • Vice versa: when atmospheric pressure increases, the boiling point increases and the melting point decreases.
🏔️ Try it — drag the altitude 0 m
Boiling point of water ≈ 100.0 °C
Worked example — Q. What is the boiling point of water at the Alps' highest peak, ≈4,800 m above sea level?

Temperature decreases 1 °C per 300 m.

Decrease = 4800 ÷ 300 = 16 °C

Boiling point = 100 − 16 = 84 °C

كل ما ارتفعنا فوق سطح البحر، الضغط الجوي بيقل، فالميه بتغلي عند حرارة أقل من 100°م — وده بيزيد نقطة انصهار الجليد فوق 0°م. والعكس صحيح: لما الضغط يزيد، الغليان بيزيد والانصهار بينزل. جرّب حرّك المؤشر شوف الحساب بنفسك.
FACTOR 02

Degree of purity

Impurities disturb the attraction forces between particles, changing the energy needed to shift state. Purity is tested by comparing a substance's melting/boiling point to its known pure value.

04 — Technological Application

The pressure cooker

A direct use of the pressure–boiling point relationship, running in reverse.

Why it saves time and fuel

  • Importance: cooks food faster than a normal pot, saving energy.
  • Idea of work: traps steam inside → pressure rises inside the pot → boiling point of water is pushed above 100 °C → food cooks faster, using less fuel.
saves 70–90% of energy vs. an open pot
قدرة الضغط بتحبس البخار جوه، فالضغط بيعلى، وده بيرفع درجة غليان الميه فوق 100°م — فالأكل بيستوي أسرع وبوقود أقل.

Ice ⇌ Water ⇌ Vapour: the two energy-gaining directions (melting, then evaporation) are the ones where particle energy rises; the reverse directions (freezing, condensation) release it.
